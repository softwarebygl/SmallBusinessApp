_Last updated: June 27, 2026_

## The short version
GL Business does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or have access to your personal or business
data. Everything you enter stays on your device and syncs through **your own private iCloud
account**. We (Gold Life LLC, the developer) cannot see it.

## What the app stores, and where
The app stores the information you enter — customers, estimates, invoices, receipts, payments,
your business profile, and any photos or receipt scans you add — **on your device and in your
private iCloud (Apple CloudKit)**. This data is controlled by you and your Apple ID. The developer
has no access to it and no copy of it.

## Camera
With your permission, the app uses your device camera **only** to capture or scan expense
receipts. Those images are processed on your device and saved with your data in your iCloud. They
are never sent to the developer or any third party.

## No account, no tracking
- There is no separate sign-up — the app uses your existing Apple ID / iCloud.
- The app contains **no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking** of any kind.

## Sharing estimates and invoices
When you choose to send an estimate or invoice, it is sent through **your own** Mail or Messages app
to the recipients **you** select. The developer does not transmit, intercept, or store these.

## Third parties
The only third-party service involved is **Apple iCloud**, which stores and syncs your data under
Apple's own privacy policy. No other third parties receive your data.

## Children
This app is not directed at children and is rated 4+.

## Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any change.

## Contact
Questions about this policy? Email **support@evolvedbygl.com**.
