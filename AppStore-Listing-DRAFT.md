## Description (≤ 4000 chars)
Business by GL is a simple, professional toolkit for running a small contracting or trades
business — built for the way you actually work: labor and materials together, job after job.

CUSTOMERS
• Keep every customer's contact info, addresses, and photo in one place.

ESTIMATES
• Build clean, grouped estimates with labor and material line items.
• Set tax (applied only to taxable items), discounts, and a required deposit.
• Add a separate job-site / service address for each estimate — perfect when a GC's office
  address stays the same but the work happens at different lots.
• Auto-numbered, with status tracking (Draft, Sent, Accepted, Declined, and auto-Expired).

INVOICES & PAYMENTS
• Turn an accepted estimate into an invoice in one tap — details carry over.
• Record payments, track the balance due, and bill deposits or milestones.
• Payment terms (due on receipt, Net 15, Net 30, or a custom date).

RECEIPTS
• Scan and store expense receipts with your camera, organized by customer/job.

SEND & SHARE
• Generate polished PDF estimates and invoices branded with your own logo.
• Send them to customers by email or text, right from your phone.

YOUR DATA, YOUR iCLOUD
• Everything syncs securely across your devices through your private iCloud.
• No separate account to create, and no third-party servers — your business data stays yours.

Make it yours: add your company logo and details, and the app brands your documents and
personalizes the app to match.
